# mmnt

**mmnt** is a simple app for taking a moment to write.

Set a timer, start writing, and don't stop until it ends. There is nothing to organize and nothing to maintain — you write to clear your mind, not to build an archive. What you wrote quietly fades away after a while, unless you choose to keep it.

## Download

1. Go to the [latest release](https://github.com/mmnt-writing/mmnt-releases/releases/latest).
2. Under **Assets**, download the `.dmg` file.
3. Open the downloaded file and drag **mmnt** into your **Applications** folder.
4. Launch mmnt from Applications.

That's it — no account, no setup. Your writing stays on your Mac and is never uploaded anywhere.

## Updates

mmnt checks for new versions when it starts and lets you know when one is available. You can also check manually anytime from **Settings**, or simply come back to this page and download the newest release.

## Requirements

- macOS 12 or later (Apple Silicon or Intel)
